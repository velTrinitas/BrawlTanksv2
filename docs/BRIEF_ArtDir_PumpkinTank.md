## PROMPT (copy-paste)

A majestic, menacing cartoon armored off-road vehicle called "PUMPKIN", game asset for a top-down tank brawler for kids 9–12. Three-quarter front view from slightly above (camera ~35° elevation, vehicle facing front-right), centered, full vehicle visible, pure white background, no ground, no text, no logo.

**Overall character:** a heavy 4-wheeled armored truck in the spirit of a military Humvee, built as if carved from a giant burnt pumpkin and dressed for the Day of the Dead. It must feel powerful, proud and a little scary — a night-parade warlord, not a cute toy. Wide, low, planted stance; strong silhouette readable at small size. NOT demonic, NOT death-cult, no blood, no gore — dark festive, like a Día de los Muertos parade float that went to war.

**Hull (body):** wide, low, rounded pumpkin-orange armored body (#E2701C), with deep vertical pumpkin ribs running front to back, giving a bulging, organic, segmented volume. The skin looks roasted and scorched: darker charred edges and fender lips, subtle cracks and scars in the rind, a soft warm highlight on the top of each rib. Rounded, swollen fenders arch over each wheel. Hull is about as tall as a wheel — compact and muscular, not a monster truck.

**Front / hood — the face:** the entire front is a large sugar-skull (calavera) mask made of aged bone (#E9DFC8, dirty ivory, slightly worn). Narrow, angular, squinting eye sockets slanted downward toward the center (stern, determined look), pitch black inside with glowing ember headlights (orange-gold light, #FF7A12) burning in the depths. Each socket is ringed by a thin dotted line of marigold petals. Inverted-heart black nose cavity. The grille is a jaw of sharp, triangular bone teeth set in a black mouth across the nose of the vehicle. Delicate magenta (#C8246E) sugar-skull swirl ornaments on the forehead, one hairline crack in the bone. A heavy black wrought-iron bull-bar curves around the front of the mask, with rivets.

**Side decoration:** along each flank a black armored band with a zig-zag marigold-yellow (#FFA41A) stitch line, like a sewn pumpkin seam. Along the top edge of the left side, a string of papel picado banners: dark charcoal flags cut with festive patterns and thin edges in magenta, marigold and teal (#1C9C92), slightly fluttering. Iron rivets on the fenders.

**Wheels:** four chunky off-road wheels (two per side), black rubber with deep chevron tread, moderate size (NOT oversized, NOT monster-truck). Hubcaps shaped like marigold flowers (deep orange petals, small magenta center).

**Rear:** a spare wheel mounted on the back with a marigold hubcap; two short, soot-blackened steel exhaust pipes.

**Turret — the jack-o'-lantern:** a round turret shaped like a large carved pumpkin with 8 deep lobes, sitting on a short dark ring. Carved face pointing forward along the gun: angry slanted triangular eyes (inner corners lower — frowning brows), a wide jagged grin with two fangs. The carvings glow from within with fire — bright yellow core, orange, deep red at the edges — casting a warm glow on the rind. A dry, gnarled, dark olive stem on the back with a curled vine tendril. Small magenta swirl and a marigold dot on the back of the pumpkin.

**Gun:** a single straight, thick cannon like a classic tank gun (not a mortar, not cartoonishly short) in dark forged iron (#2E2420), with one reinforcing ring wrapped in a marigold-yellow band, and a boxy muzzle brake with three horizontal slots at the tip. A faint ember glow inside the muzzle.

**Palette:** dominant scorched pumpkin orange; bone ivory; charcoal black and dark iron; accents of marigold yellow, magenta and teal used sparingly as festive ornaments. Warm ember glows are the only light sources on the vehicle.

**Rendering style:** high-detail cartoon game art, bold clean dark outlines (thick on the silhouette, thinner inside), cel-shaded volumes with soft gradients, strong rim light, crisp specular highlights on bone and metal, chunky proportions, toy-like readability but premium finish — exactly matching the existing Brawl Tanks roster portraits (same line weight, same lighting from top-left, same level of detail). Soft contact shadow directly under the wheels only.

**Avoid:** realism, photo textures, horror gore, demons, horns, pentagrams, blood, flames covering the vehicle, text/letters, flags of countries, cute/baby proportions, oversized wheels, background scenery.
